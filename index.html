CRAN R Packages

Individually developed and maintained an official CRAN package implementing the Mapper algorithm in R. SimplicialComplex: Construction & Homology Features

Individually developed and maintained an official CRAN package that constructs simplicial complexes and computes persistent homology features, bringing TDA tooling to the R ecosystem, where such implementations remain scarce.
