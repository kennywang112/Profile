Selected Research Projects

Research & Development | 2023 - Present

Interdependency Modelling of Railway Infrastructure Systems | Master Research
Formulated an Isotonic Tree-Augmented Naive Bayes (TAN) framework to model spatio-temporal dependencies in railway drainage and track infrastructure along the London–Cardiff corridor, quantifying critical drivers behind prolonged flooding and cascading economic disruption.

Visualizing High-Dimensional Data with Topological Data Analysis: A Case Study on Traffic Accident Risk | Bachelor Research
Pioneered a white-box dimensional reduction framework via the Mapper algorithm to untangle multi-dimensional accident heterogeneity across Taiwan's roadway networks, coupling topological representations with ML models for explainable risk identification.

Integrating Spatial Topology and Exogenous Variables for Traffic Crash Forecasting: A Case Study of Greater London
Evaluated spatio-temporal deep learning architectures against spatial econometric models, uncovering spatial and temporal dependencies of collision hotspots via Moran's I, Getis-Ord Gi*, ACF, and Emerging Hot Spot Analysis.

Data Centre Site Selection in England Using MaxEnt Model
Engineered a spatial multi-criteria decision analysis (MCDA) framework using Maximum Entropy (MaxEnt) modeling across England, synthesizing brownfield availability, drought severity, geology, demographics, and thermal constraints.
